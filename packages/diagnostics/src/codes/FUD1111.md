# FUD1111 — `<link rel="server">` without a `.fudapi`
**error** · SDD-60

A `<link rel="server">` brings a contract into a route or a component: its `href` must name an existing `.fudapi`, usually the sibling of the `.fud` (`./usuarios.fudapi`).
**Fix:** point `href` at the `.fudapi`, or create it.
