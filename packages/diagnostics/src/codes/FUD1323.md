# FUD1323 — `@catch` without its `@await`
**error** · SDD-70

`@catch` is what an `@await` renders when its promise fails, so it belongs to one `@await`: it follows that `@await`'s closing brace, and an `@await` has only one.
**Fix:** put `@catch (e) { … }` right after the closing brace of its `@await`.
