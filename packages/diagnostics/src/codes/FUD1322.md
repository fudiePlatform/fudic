# FUD1322 — `@await` outside the body of a route or a page
**error** · SDD-70

`data` exists only in the view of a route or a page, and the `<head>` is the first thing sent: it never waits. A component, a layout or a snippet receives its values already resolved.
**Fix:** move the `@await` to the route's own view, below the `<head>`; a component receives the resolved value as a prop: `@await data.users { <user-table .rows=@data.users></user-table> }`.
