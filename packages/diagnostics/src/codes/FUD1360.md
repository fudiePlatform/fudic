# FUD1360 — `fails` under a function or `load`
**error** · SDD-74

`fails` ties a rule to its text and names what a middleware rejects with. A function or `load` has nothing to tie: its handler is code, and the error it throws already carries its status and its text, translated with `t()` like any other text of a `.ts`.
**Fix:** remove the line and throw from the handler: `throw conflict(t("…"))`. Add `catches <status>` to the function to keep that status on the page.
