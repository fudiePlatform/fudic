# FUD1114 — A component links a page's contract
**error** · SDD-60

`load`, `paths` and `layout` belong to the route the `.fudapi` sits next to. A component may link a `.fudapi` for its functions and forms, never one that declares a page.
**Fix:** link a `.fudapi` without `load`, `paths` or `layout`, or move what the component needs into one.
