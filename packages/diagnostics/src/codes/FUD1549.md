# FUD1549 — This referrer policy leaves `back` empty
**warning**

`no-referrer`, `origin` and `strict-origin` do not send the path of the previous page within the same origin. fudic reads it to know where `back` returns, so `back` falls to its default.
**Fix:** use `strict-origin-when-cross-origin` (the template's), `same-origin` or `origin-when-cross-origin`.
