# FUD1516 — A name the route's fixture does not have
**error**

`fudic_render` and `fudic_preview` paint a route with its fixture: `data` names entries of its `data`, `answers` groups of its `calls`, `services` a group of its `services`. One of the names asked is not in the route's fixture, so nothing is painted with a guess.
**Fix:** ask for one of the names the message lists, or add that entry to the fixture.
