# FUD1550 — HSTS preload without what it requires
**warning**

`Strict-Transport-Security` asks for `preload`, but the browsers' preload list only takes a `max-age` of one year or more together with `includeSubDomains`.
**Fix:** raise `max-age` to `31536000` or more and add `includeSubDomains`, or remove `preload`.
