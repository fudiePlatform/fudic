# FUD1151 — A condition that is not a boolean
**error** · SDD-61

What counts as true for a string, a number or a list differs from one language to another (`""`, `0`, an empty list). Conditions, the arrow of `filter` and the operands of `&&`, `||` and `!` are booleans.
**Fix:** compare explicitly: `x !== null`, `n > 0`.
