# FUD1240 — Only a route or a component has a server
**error**

`fudic g api` writes the `.fudapi`, the link and the handler of a route or a component. A layout, a snippet or a file that is not a `.fud` of one of them has no server of its own. Nothing is written.
**Fix:** a layout reads its data through its route's `load`; a snippet through its caller.
