# FUD0970 — Not a component
**error**

The workbench opens a component — a `.fud` whose markup is its own tag wrapping a `<template shadowrootmode>` — or a `.fudspec`: a component's, a page's (`page <route>`) or a flow's (`flow <name>`). A layout, or a route with no `.fudspec` of its own, has nothing to run.
**Fix:** open the workbench from a component, from a `.fudspec`, or from a route that has one beside it.
