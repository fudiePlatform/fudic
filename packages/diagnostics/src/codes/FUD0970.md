# FUD0970 — Not a component
**error** · SDD-32

The workbench mounts one component on a page of its own, so it opens a `.fud` whose markup is its own tag wrapping a `<template shadowrootmode>`, or that component's `.fudspec`. A page, a route or a layout cannot be mounted that way.
**Fix:** open the workbench from a component or from its `.fudspec`.
