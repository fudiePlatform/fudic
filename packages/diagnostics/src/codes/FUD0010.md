# FUD0010 — `@` starts no Razor construct
**error** · SDD-03

In markup, `@` always opens Razor: an expression, a control construct, `@code` or a comment. The character after this one opens none of them.
**Fix:** write the construct you meant (`@name`, `@(a + b)`, `@if (…)`), or escape a literal at sign as `@@`.
