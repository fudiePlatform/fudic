# FUD1112 — `<link rel="server">` in a layout or a snippet
**error** · SDD-60

Only a route or a component links a `.fudapi`. A layout receives its props from the route (`layout` in the route's `.fudapi`), and a snippet renders what its caller gives it.
**Fix:** move the link, and what it brings, to the route or component that uses the layout or the snippet.
