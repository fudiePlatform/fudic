# FUD1320 — `@await` needs a `later` field of `data`
**error** · SDD-70

`@await` waits for a value that `load` returns as a promise, so what follows it is the read of a field marked `later` in the route's `.fudapi`, as is: `data.users`. A resolved field, a signal, a call or any other expression has nothing to wait for.
**Fix:** mark the field `later` in the route's `.fudapi` (`users  Person[]  later`) and await it by name: `@await data.users { … }`.
