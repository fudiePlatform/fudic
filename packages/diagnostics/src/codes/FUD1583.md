# FUD1583 — The runtime is the layout's
**error**

A `<script type="module" src="fudic:runtime">` outside the `<head>` of a layout: in a route, a component, or the `<body>` of a layout. The layout owns the document, and the marker in its `<head>` is where fudic writes the scripts of every page that links it; anywhere else it is written as it is and the page gets no runtime.
**Fix:** delete it from there and keep it as a direct child of the `<head>` of the layout.
