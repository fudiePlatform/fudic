# FUD1382 — Delegated row clashes with a name in scope
**error** · SDD-72

The handler reads `day`, which is the row a `delegate:day` below hands over, but `day` is also declared where the handler is written: in the `@code`, by an import, or by a loop around it. fudic does not pick one for you.
**Fix:** rename the loop binding (`@foreach (const d of days)` with `delegate:d`) or the other variable.
