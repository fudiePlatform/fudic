# FUD1382 — Delegated row clashes with a name in scope
**error**

The handler reads `day`, which is the row a `delegate:day` below hands over, but `day` is also declared by a loop around the handler. fudic does not pick one for you. (A clash with a name of `@code` or an import is `FUD0918`, on the loop.)
**Fix:** rename the inner binding and put the handler outside the loops, where it is one listener: `<section @click=@pick(d)>` around them, `@foreach (const d of days)` with `delegate:d`.
