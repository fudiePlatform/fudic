# FUD1080 — Unknown name
**error** · SDD-60

A type, rule, middleware, pattern or form named in a `.fudapi` must be declared somewhere in the project: in a `.fudapi` of the app or in the `fudapi/` folder of a direct dependency. `number` is not a type: numbers say their width (`u8 i8 u16 i16 u32 i32 f32 f64`).
**Fix:** correct the name (the message offers the closest ones), or declare it.
