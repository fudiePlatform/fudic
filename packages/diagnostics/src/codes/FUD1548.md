# FUD1548 — The Service Worker policy blocks its linker
**warning**

The `Content-Security-Policy` of `sw.headers` does not let in what the Service Worker needs to link the route chunks (`'unsafe-eval'`). That is legal: the worker finds out it cannot link and the app is served without it, by the server. Rarely what is wanted.
**Fix:** add the source the message names to `script-src` (the bulb does it), or remove `sw.json` if the app is meant to have no Service Worker.
