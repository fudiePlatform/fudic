# FUD1140 — A call in the view
**error** · SDD-61

The view is lowered to every backend language, so it calls nothing but a reactive read (`count()`, `form.$valid()`) and the collection operations `filter`, `map`, `reduce` and `toSorted`. Methods such as `find`, `join` or `toFixed`, and functions of the `@code`, exist only in JS.
**Fix:** write it with `filter`, `map`, `reduce`, `toSorted` and `.length`, or compute it in `load()` or a pipe.
