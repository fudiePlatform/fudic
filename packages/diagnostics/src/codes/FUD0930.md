# FUD0930 — Block out of order
**error**

A criterion opens with at most one `given`, and then `when` and `then` take turns as many times as it needs: do something, check it, do something else, check that. A `given` after another block, or two blocks of the same kind in a row, breaks the turns. The out-of-place block's terms are still parsed.
**Fix:** merge two blocks in a row into one, or move the `given` to the top.
