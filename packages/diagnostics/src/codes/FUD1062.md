# FUD1062 — Unknown declaration
**error** · SDD-60

Every line at level 0 of a `.fudapi` starts a declaration: a verb, `load`, `paths`, `layout`, `chain`, `type`, `enum`, `form`, `rule`, `middleware`, `pattern` or `messages`. This one starts with something else, and what hangs below it is skipped.
**Fix:** start the line with one of those words, or indent it under the declaration it belongs to.
