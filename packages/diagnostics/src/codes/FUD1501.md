# FUD1501 — A line for another kind of spec
**error**

Each kind of spec mounts something different, and its fixture lines follow: `props` feeds a component, `data` and `path` feed a page's `load` and URL, `answers`, `services` and `url` serve pages and flows. A line of another kind has nothing to feed here.
**Fix:** remove the line, or move the criterion to a spec of the kind it belongs to.
