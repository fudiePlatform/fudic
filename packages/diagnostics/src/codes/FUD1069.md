# FUD1069 — Malformed expression
**error** · SDD-60

The expression of a `require` or a `when` is made of field paths (`access.key`), literals, `== != < <= > >=`, `and or not`, `contains`, `valid c`, `empty c`, `length c` and parentheses. This one stops making sense at the token the message names.
**Fix:** complete or reorder the expression: `empty alias or alias != name`.
