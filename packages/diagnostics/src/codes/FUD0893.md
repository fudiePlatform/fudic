# FUD0893 — Layout hole inside a construct
**error** · SDD-48

A `@RenderBody()` or `@RenderSection()` is a fixed point of the layout: inside a branch the route may not be written at all, inside a loop it would be written many times, and the route's chunk would not find its own nodes.
**Fix:** keep the hole outside `@if`/`@switch`/loops and do the branching inside the route.
