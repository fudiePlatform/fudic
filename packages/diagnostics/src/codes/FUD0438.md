# FUD0438 — Framework link not at the top level
**error** · SDD-21

In a component or a route, `<link rel="component|layout|snippet">` is read from the top level only; nested (even inside the `<head>` fragment) it registers nothing and its `.fud` would be published as an asset.
**Fix:** move the link to the top of the file, before `@code`.
