# FUD1410 — Recorded without a text
**error**

An `accepted` or a `reopened` line says why, in the words of the person who decided it: that the client accepted the piece, or which change of its contract opens it again. Without the text the history would say that something happened and not what, so nothing was recorded.
**Fix:** call `fudic_record` again with `text`: what the user said, in their words.
