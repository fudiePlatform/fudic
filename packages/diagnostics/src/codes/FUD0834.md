# FUD0834 — Two snippets under one name
**error** · SDD-29

A `@render` finds its snippet by name, so every name in a file's scope — its own declarations plus everything its `<link rel="snippet">` imports bring — must be unique.
**Fix:** rename one of them, or import one under a namespace: `<link rel="snippet" href="./cards.fud" as="cards">`.
