# FUD1300 — A prerendered page reads the session
**error** · SDD-69

A prerender has no request: its cookies are empty and its HTML is written once for everyone. A `load` or a `layout` that reads `ctx.cookies`, or the `cookie` or `authorization` header, would freeze the page as «nobody is signed in» for every visitor.
**Fix:** a page that depends on who is asking is rendered per request: write `load ssr`.
