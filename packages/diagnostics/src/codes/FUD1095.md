# FUD1095 — Bad field or types in a `require`
**error** · SDD-60

A `require` reads the fields of its form (or of its group, in a group's `summary`) by their path: `access.key`. Comparisons take two values of one type, `<`/`>` numbers or dates, `contains` a text or a list, and `and`/`or`/`not` yes/no values.
**Fix:** correct the path, or compare values of the same type.
