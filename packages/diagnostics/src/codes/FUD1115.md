# FUD1115 — A route links another route's `load`
**error** · SDD-60

The `data` of a route comes from the `load` of its own `.fudapi`, the one next to it. Linking another route's `.fudapi` would bring a second `load`, with the params of a different page.
**Fix:** link the route's own `.fudapi`; move shared functions or forms to a `.fudapi` without `load`.
