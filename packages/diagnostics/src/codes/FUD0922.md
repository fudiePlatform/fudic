# FUD0922 — Missing header
**error**

Every `.fudspec` says on its first line what its criteria open: `component <tag>` mounts a component alone, `page <route>` opens a route with its layout, and `flow <name>` opens nothing, each criterion starting at its own URL.
**Fix:** add `component prefix-name`, `page /route` or `flow name` at the top of the file.
