# FUD1570 — Two error pages in the chain
**error**

The app has no `src/routes/_error.fud` of its own, and two or more libraries of its dependency chain have a `src/_error.fud`.
fudic never picks one by order: adding a dependency would change the app's pages without a word.
**Fix:** write the app's own error page with `fudic g error-page`; it wins over every library's.
