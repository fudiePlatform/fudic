# FUD1562 — A declaration hides a lifecycle hook
**error**

The seven lifecycle hooks (`beforeHydrate`, `afterHydrate`, `beforeCreate`, `afterCreate`, `onConnect`, `onDisconnect`, `onDispose`) are part of `@code` like `props`: they are not imported, and the compiler knows them by name. A `const`, a `function`, a parameter or an import with one of those names would make a call to it ambiguous.
**Fix:** rename your declaration, or drop the import: the hook needs none.
