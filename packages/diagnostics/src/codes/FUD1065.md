# FUD1065 — Line not valid under its parent
**error** · SDD-60

What a `.fudapi` line can say is decided by the line it hangs from: a function takes `use`, `skip`, `form`, `fails`, arguments and `->`; a form takes options, fields and `summary`; and so on. This line starts with a word its parent does not take.
**Fix:** use one of the words the message lists, or move the line under the declaration it belongs to.
