# FUD1517 — The route has no fixture
**error**

A route was asked to be painted with its fixture, and there is no `<route>.fixture.ts` beside its `.fud`. The fixture of a page is the one its `.fudspec` uses, its unit tests apply and the MCP paints with.
**Fix:** write the fixture (`fudic g spec /<route>` writes one with a sample `data`), or paint the route without one.
