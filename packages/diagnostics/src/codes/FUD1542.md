# FUD1542 — A header fudic sends itself
**error**

The header is one fudic decides: `Content-Type`, `Content-Length`, `Transfer-Encoding`, `Location` and `Set-Cookie` everywhere, `Link` on pages (Early Hints), `Service-Worker-Allowed` and `Cache-Control` on `/fudic-sw.js`. Or a block of `assets.headers` names `/fudic-sw.js` or the manifest, which are always `no-cache`.
**Fix:** remove the line or the block.
