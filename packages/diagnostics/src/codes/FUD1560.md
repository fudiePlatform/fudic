# FUD1560 — Lifecycle hook outside the top level of `@code`
**error**

`beforeHydrate`, `afterHydrate`, `beforeCreate`, `afterCreate`, `onConnect`, `onDisconnect` and `onDispose` register code that runs at a moment of the component's life. They are registered once per instance, while `@code` runs, so a call inside a function, a handler, an `if`, a loop or a callback registers nothing the framework can count on.
**Fix:** move the call to the top level of `@code`, as a statement of its own; put the condition inside the function you pass to it.
