# FUD0741 — Two stylesheets under one name
**error** · SDD-42

A stylesheet is adopted by its name, and a name is one sheet in the module map. The same name is declared twice — in `globalStyles` and `styles` of one `fudic.json`, or by two packages of one dependency chain — so one sheet would silently replace the other.
**Fix:** rename one of the two sheets.
