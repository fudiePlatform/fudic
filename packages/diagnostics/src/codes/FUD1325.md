# FUD1325 — `buffer` needs a whole number of bytes
**error** · SDD-70

`buffer` on the `load` line says how many bytes the page's stream may hold before it sends them. It is a whole number, `0` or more, with an optional `k` for kilobytes: `buffer 0`, `buffer 4096`, `buffer 16k`.
**Fix:** write the bytes the stream may hold before it sends, `buffer 0` to send every piece at once or `buffer 16k`.
