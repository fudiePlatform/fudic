# FUD1321 — A `later` field read outside its `@await`
**error** · SDD-70

A field marked `later` is a promise until its `@await` resolves it: only inside the body of `@await data.<field>` is it the value. Read anywhere else, or handed whole to a prop or to `@code`, it is still pending.
**Fix:** read it inside `@await data.users { … }`; everything that needs the value goes in that block.
