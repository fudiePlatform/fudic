# FUD1563 — A layout hook reads a prop of the layout
**error**

A layout's props are resolved on the server, by the `layout` of its route's `.fudapi`, and painted into the head. Its lifecycle hooks run in the browser, in the route's JavaScript, where those props have no value.
**Fix:** read what the hook needs from the page itself (the `<body>` it receives, an attribute the layout wrote), or move the hook to the route.
