# FUD0700 — A layout's `@code` holds more than its props
**error** · SDD-40 · SDD-60

A layout's `@code` only declares its props with `props<{…}>()`. A loose statement or a `signal` has no place to run: a layout has no `load`, links no `.fudapi` and has no client half.
**Fix:** move the logic to the route, whose `.fudapi` resolves the layout's props with `layout`.
