# FUD0700 — A layout's `@code` holds more than its props
**error** · SDD-40

A layout's `@code` only declares its props with `props<{…}>()`. A `@server` or `@client` region, a loose statement or a `signal` has no place to run: a layout has no `load` and no client half.
**Fix:** move the logic to the route, which resolves the layout's props in `export function layout(ctx, data)`.
