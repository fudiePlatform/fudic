# FUD1547 — The page policy lets more in than it needs
**warning**

A source the browser does use weakens the `Content-Security-Policy` of `page.headers`: `'unsafe-eval'`, a scheme or a host in `script-src` without `'strict-dynamic'`, `'unsafe-inline'` without a nonce. Or a directive that closes a door is missing: `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri`, `frame-ancestors`.
**Fix:** remove the source, put `'strict-dynamic'` before it, or add the missing directive as the template has it.
