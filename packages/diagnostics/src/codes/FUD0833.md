# FUD0833 — `@` inside a `@render` header
**retired** · SDD-48

It rejected a `@` in the arguments of a `@render`; decision 135 made the opposite rule, uniform with props: an argument that reads the scope is written with its `@`.
**Replaced by:** FUD0894 (argument without its `@`) and FUD0895 (`@` not followed by a path).
